# SlateIQ — Plan (Agentic Cinema Hackathon, ClickHouse track)

## One-liner
**SlateIQ turns a day of raw dailies into a queryable production brain.** A Gemini-powered multi-agent
network (Google ADK) watches every take, writes structured, timestamped knowledge into ClickHouse, and
then answers the questions editors, script supervisors, 1st ADs and producers ask every night —
through the official ClickHouse MCP server. It also autonomously produces the industry documents
(Daily Progress Report, Editor's Log / circled-take list, Continuity alerts) that today are typed by hand at 1 a.m.

## Why this wins (judging criteria)

| **Idea** | Non-obvious: treat a film shoot as a *time-series/event data problem* (takes, lines, flags, camera metadata) and give production a real analytical DB + agents; "asks your footage" and "asks your schedule" in one place. |

## Users & core questions (drive the demo)
- **Editor / assistant editor:** "Best takes for scene 12?", "Every take where Celia says 'forty years'?", "Takes with boom in shot or soft focus?", "Give me an EDL-ish list of circled takes for today."
- **Script supervisor:** auto continuity notes across takes; line-reading differences vs. script.
- **1st AD / UPM / Producer:** "Are we on schedule? Pages shot vs. planned, shooting ratio by scene, which scenes are at risk, overtime trend, camera hours." → Daily Progress Report generated + spoken.
- **Director:** "Show me the 3 takes with the most emotionally intense delivery in scene 40."

## Architecture
```
 dailies clips (mp4)  ──►  ingest/ (Gemini 3.5 flash multimodal, structured JSON) ──► ClickHouse
 call sheet / script  ──►  ingest/ (Gemini doc parsing)                            ──► ClickHouse
 synthetic 30-day shoot telemetry (frame-level, camera, audio levels) ────────────► ClickHouse (millions of rows)

 ClickHouse  ◄── mcp-clickhouse (HTTP, official) ◄── ADK agents (McpToolset)
                                                        ├─ coordinator (router, memory of schema)
                                                        ├─ editor_agent (take search, circled takes)
                                                        ├─ production_agent (schedule, DPR, risk)
                                                        ├─ continuity_agent (cross-take conflicts)
                                                        └─ report_agent (DPR / Editor's Log markdown → TTS)
 Web UI (React) ── FastAPI (ADK get_fast_api_app) ── Cloud Run
 Grafana (Cloud Run, provisioned, ClickHouse datasource) embedded panels for production health
```
Hosting (free tier): ClickHouse + mcp-clickhouse on 1× e2-micro (us-central1) behind Caddy w/ bearer token;
agent+UI on Cloud Run (min 0); clips on GCS public bucket; Grafana on Cloud Run.

## Data model (see db/SCHEMA.md — the contract)
production, shooting_day (call sheet: scenes planned, call time, wrap), scene (script: number, pages/8ths, INT/EXT, DAY/NIGHT, characters, synopsis), take (scene, shot, take no, camera roll, tc_in/out, duration, status circled/ng, director note), take_event (ts-offset events: dialogue line w/ speaker & text, action, flag {soft_focus, boom, flub, overlap, continuity}, emotion score), continuity_note, frame_telemetry (per-second: focus_score, exposure, audio_peak_db, motion) — the big table, camera_metadata.

## Footage
Tears of Steel (Blender Foundation, CC-BY 3.0) 720p at data/footage/tos.mp4 → split into ~24 “takes” across ~8 scenes by ffmpeg scene detection; some clips duplicated with ffmpeg degradations (blur = soft focus, gain = audio clip, crop = boom) to produce realistic QC variety. Attribution in README.
